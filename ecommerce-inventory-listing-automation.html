E-Commerce Inventory & Product Listing Automation

Your inventory lives in portals, spreadsheets, vendor feeds and browser sessions nobody wants to log into by hand. BidBuddy LLC builds the pipeline that turns all of it into live, current, correctly-priced listings — on your own store and queued for every marketplace you sell on.

The pipeline we build (fixed scope, fixed price)

1. Feed & session fetching — n8n workflows that pull inventory from portals, vendor sites and spreadsheets; authenticated browser-session runners handle sources with no clean API. Idempotent pulls, duplicate-source detection, logged runs.

2. Filtering & enrichment — availability rules, zero-photo skip logic, location/branch geocoding, and pricing formulas (e.g. custom median calculations) applied per item — your business rules encoded exactly, versioned, and reviewable.

3. Publishing — qualifying units publish straight to your web store (Cloudflare, Shopify, WooCommerce or custom API) with clean multi-photo gallery mapping into your listing API payload — normalized indexes, empty-array guards, no broken galleries.

4. Marketplace queue — draft listings for social marketplaces and classifieds queue up for your review before anything goes public, with dedupe and last-write-wins checks so the same item never posts twice.

5. Reliability pass — batch tuning on large item sets, per-item retries, failure alerts. One bad record never stalls the night's run — and you get a pass/fail report, not silence.

Built to be handed off

Every workflow we ship is documented and exportable. Loop logic, payload schemas and error handling are commented inside the n8n flow itself, so your team owns it after we leave. Stress-tested on batch runs before delivery, verified end-to-end on a real item.
